# Pratika Sport visual notes

This addition follows the current site system; it does not introduce a new brand direction.

- **Palette:** deep blue-green `#103d4e` for headings, action blue `#006cae`, Pratika green `#55971f`, pale green `#eaf4df`, and white for photo surfaces.
- **Type:** Geist Sans, with compact balanced headings and comfortable paragraph line-height.
- **Photo presentation:** real project photos remain distinct from conceptual illustrations. Keep original files, use descriptive alt text, reserve image dimensions, and use AVIF/WebP responsive variants.
- **Project cards:** soft white-to-pale-green surface, clear completed-status label, place name, two real photo angles, and a restrained note when images receive digital treatment.
- **Responsive behavior:** project images sit side by side; the card stacks text under the images below 700px. Preserve keyboard focus and sufficient text contrast.
